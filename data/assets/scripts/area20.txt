	PrintClear "'WHAT A BUNCH OF CHEAPSKATES,' THE LEADER COMPLAINS. 'LET 'EM HAVE IT BOYS!'"
	Goto FightPatrol

	PrintClear "THEY PAW THROUGH YOUR COINS AND LEAVE."
	Rob 1 50 0
	Picture 255
	Exit

SurrenderToPatrol:
	PrintClear "THE MONSTERS ROB AND BLINDFOLD YOU. YOU ARE KICKED SOUNDLY AND SENT ON YOUR WAY."
	Rob 1 100 100
	Gosub PressReturn
	Goto RunAway

TryRunning:
	CheckParty SelMove 0 Scratch9 Scratch1 Scratch1 Scratch1
	Compare AuxEncResult Scratch1
	IfLt
	Goto RunAway
	IfGt
	Goto FightPatrol
	IfEq
	Random 1 Scratch1
	Compare Scratch1 1
	IfEq
	Goto RunAway
	Goto FightPatrol

PatrolWarnsLeave:
	Compare AuxParlay 95
	IfGt
	Goto PatrolAdvice
	PrintClear "THE LEADER SPEAKS, 'THE BOSS DON'T ALLOW YOUR KIND IN HIS TERRITORY.  LEAVE NOW OR WE'LL HAVE TO RUN YOU IN.' (WHAT DO YOU DO?)"
	HMenu Scratch9 4 "FIGHT" "STAY" "LEAVE" "RUN"
	OnGoto Scratch9 4 FightPatrol FightPatrol Done RunAway

PatrolAdvice:
	PrintClear "'LET ME GIVE YOU A PIECE OF ADVICE,' THEIR LEADER SAYS.  'STAY AWAY FROM THE TEXTILE HOUSE. THE UNDEAD ARE GOING CRAZY UP THERE.'  THE MONSTERS TURN TO LEAVE. (WHAT DO YOU DO?)"
	HMenu Scratch9 2 "LET THEM GO" "STOP THEM"
	OnGoto Scratch9 2 Done StopPatrol

StopPatrol:
	PrintClear "'HOW DARE YOU INTERFERE WITH THE BOSS'S BUSINESS?' THE LEADER ROARS.  'KILL THEM,' HE ORDERS."
	Gosub PressReturn
	Goto FightPatrol

	Compare AuxParlay 65
	IfLt
	Add PatrolMorale 20 PatrolMorale
	Gosub PressReturn
	Goto FightPatrol
