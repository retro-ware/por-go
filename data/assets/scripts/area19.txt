event0:
	Exit

event1:
	Picture 12
	AndOr DragonCaveFlags 128 Scratch2
	Compare Scratch2 1
	IfNe
	Goto CheckCaveState

EmptyCaveLeave:
	Print "YOU ENTER THE ENORMOUS CAVE, SEARCH IT AND FIND NOTHING OF INTREST.  YOU THEN LEAVE."
	NewECL 25

CheckCaveState:
	AndOr DragonCaveFlags 2 Scratch2
	IfNe
	Goto EmptyCaveLeave
	AndOr DragonCaveFlags 1 Scratch2
	Compare Scratch2 1
	IfNe
	Goto MagicMouthAsks
	FindItem 82
	IfEq
	Goto BottleRecovered

MagicMouthAsks:
	Print "AS YOU ARE ENTERING THE CAVE, A MAGIC MOUTH APPEARS ON ONE OF THE WALLS.  IN A BOOMING VOICE IT SAYS, 'WHO ENTERS THE HOME OF DIOGENES?  SPEAK NOW AND DECLARE YOUR INTENT!'"
	HMenu Scratch1 2 "STATE NAME AND INTENT" "LEAVE"
	OnGoto Scratch1 2 StateIntentMenu LeaveCave

StateIntentMenu:
	Print "WHAT WILL YOU SAY IS YOUR REASON FOR BEING HERE?"
	Gosub PressReturn
	VMenu Scratch1 "DRAGON'S CAVE" 4 "ASK DRAGON FOR ADVICE" "CHALLENGE EVIL LEADER OF OLD PHLAN" "PLEDGE PARTY TO DRAGON'S SERVICE" "OFFER A GIFT"
	OnGoto Scratch1 4 AskAdvice ChallengeDragon PledgeService OfferGift

PledgeService:
	Picture 117
	Print "YOU HEAR A GREAT FLAPPING SOUND.  A STRONG WHOOSH OF AIR COMES DOWN UPON THE ADVENTURERS.  A LARGE SILVER DRAGON STANDS BEFORE YOU.  IN A BOOMING VOICE IT SAYS..."
	Gosub PressReturn
	Print "'I ACCEPT YOUR PLEDGE.  FOR YOUR FIRST DUTY, I COMMAND YOU TO RECOVER A POWERFUL MAGICAL BOTTLE FROM A KOBOLD SETTLEMENT IN THE HILLS FAR TO THE EAST.  THEN RETURN TO ME AND I SHALL GIVE YOU ANOTHER TASK.'"

DragonFliesOff:
	Gosub PressReturn
	Print "AFTER SPEAKING, THE DRAGON FLYS THROUGH AN OPENNING IN THE CEILING OF THE CAVE.  YOU EXIT THE CAVE TO CONTINUE YOUR QUEST."
	AndOr DragonCaveFlags 1 DragonCaveFlags
	Goto LeaveCave

ChallengeDragon:
	Picture 117
	Print "YOU HEAR A ROAR THEN A GREAT FLAPPING SOUND.  A STRONG RUSH OF AIR BATTERS THE PARTY AS A LARGE SILVER DRAGON LANDS BEFORE THE ADVENTURERS.  IN A BOOMING VOICE IT SAYS..."
	Gosub PressReturn
	Print "'YOU FOOLS.  I AM A GOOD SILVER DRAGON NOT THE EVIL LEADER OF PHLAN.  DON'T YOU KNOW THAT WE METALLIC DRAGONS ARE GOOD CREATURES?'  WHAT WILL YOU DO?"
	HMenu Scratch1 2 "APOLOGIZE" "SLAY DRAGON"
	OnGoto Scratch1 2 ApologizeToDragon DragonFleesUp

ApologizeToDragon:
	Print "AFTER HEARING YOUR APOLOGY THE DRAGON SAYS, 'THOUGH YOU ARE FOOLISH I BELIEVE YOUR ACTS ARE NOBLE.  I WILL HELP YOU WITH YOUR QUEST TO CLEANSE THE CITY OF PHLAN FROM EVIL.'"
	Gosub PressReturn
	Print "FAR TO THE EAST THERE IS A LARGE KOBOLD COMMUNITY.  THEY HAVE POSSESION OF A POWERFUL SILVER BOTTLE.  THIS BOTTLE WILL HELP YOU RID THE CITY OF ITS UNDEAD PROBLEM.  GET THE BOTTLE THEN RETURN TO ME."
	Goto DragonFliesOff

DragonFleesUp:
	Print "AS YOU READY YOUR WEAPONS TO ATTACK.  THE DRAGON TAKES FLIGHT AND ROARS, 'EVIL CREATURES, BEGONE FROM MY LAIR.'  HE THEN FLIES UP THROUGH A HOLE IN THE CEILING."
	Gosub PressReturn
	Print "YOU MAY EASILY FOLLOW THE DRAGON BY CLIMBING UP A WALL AND THEN THROUGH THE HOLE IN THE CEILING.  WHAT WILL YOU DO?"
	HMenu Scratch1 2 "FOLLOW DRAGON" "LEAVE"
	OnGoto Scratch1 2 FollowDragonUp LeaveCave

FollowDragonUp:
	Print "YOU CLIMB TO THE SECOND LEVEL OF THE DRAGON'S DEN.  THERE IS THE DRAGON WAITING FOR YOU.  HE LOOKS EXTREMELY MAD!  WHAT WILL YOU DO?"
	HMenu Scratch1 2 "ATTACK" "LEAVE"
	OnGoto Scratch1 2 AttackDragonFireball LeaveCave

AttackDragonFireball:
	Print "YOU TRY TO ATTACK THE DRAGON BUT SEEM TO BE FROZEN IN TIME.  THE DRAGON THROWS A FIREBALL IN THE MIDDLE OF THE PARTY AND THEN DISAPPEARS.  YOU HAVE A STRANGE FEELING THAT YOU JUST MADE A BIG MISTAKE."
	Damage 192 11 6 0 4
	Print "AFTER RECOVERING FROM THE FIREBALL YOU SEARCH THE REST OF THE LAIR AND FIND NOTHING OF INTREST."
	AndOr DragonCaveFlags 128 DragonCaveFlags
	Goto LeaveCave

AskAdvice:
	Picture 117
	Print "THE CHARACTERS HEAR A LOUD FLAPPING NOISE AND THEN A WARM RUSH OF AIR HITS THEIR FACES.  STANDING BEFORE YOU NOW IS A GREAT SILVER DRAGON.  HE SAYS, 'YOU ARE WISE TO SEEK MY COUNCIL AND I CAN HELP YOU...'"
	Gosub PressReturn
	Print "'FAR TO THE EAST THERE IS A LARGE KOBOLD COMPLEX.  THERE IS A SILVER BOTTLE OF GREAT POWER THERE THAT WILL HELP YOU ON YOUR QUEST.  RETRIEVE THAT BOTTLE THEN RETURN AND I WILL SEND YOU ON ANOTHER QUEST.'"
	Goto DragonFliesOff

OfferGift:
	Picture 117
	Print "THE CHARACTERS HEAR A LOUD FLAPPING NOISE AND THEN FEEL A WARM JET OF AIR HIT THEIR FACES.  A HUGE SILVER DRAGON FLIES DOWN TO MEET THEM."
	Gosub PressReturn
	Print "THE DRAGON LANDS, LOOKS THE PARTY OVER THEN SAYS 'YOU HAVE NOTHING THAT INTRESTS ME.  IF YOU WISH TO APEASE ME THEN GO EAST AND DESTROY THE KOBOLD COMMUNITY THERE.'"
	Gosub PressReturn
	Print "'THERE YOU SHALL FIND A MAGICAL BOTTLE WHICH WILL HELP YOU IN CLEANSING OLD PHLAN OF ITS EVIL!'"
	Goto DragonFliesOff

BottleRecovered:
	Print "AS YOU ENTER THE CAVE YOU ARE GREETED BY DIOGENES, THE SILVER DRAGON.  HE SAYS 'I SEE YOU HAVE RECOVERED THE SILVER BOTTLE FROM THE KOBOLD CAVES.  I CONGRADULATE YOU.'"
	Gosub PressReturn
	Print "'THAT BOTTLE CONTAINS A POWERFUL EFREET WHO WAS SUMMONED TO KILL VAMPIRES.  HE WILL BE A VALUABLE ALLY IN OLD PHLAN.  IN VALHINGEN GRAVEYARD THERE IS A VAMPIRE.'"
	Gosub PressReturn
	Print "'WHEN YOU MEET THE VAMPIRE USE THE BOTTLE AND YOU MAY HAVE A CHANCE IN DEFEATING HIM.  I WISH YOU LUCK.'  AFTER SPEAKING DIOGENES FLIES THROUGH A HOLE IN THE CEILING AND DISSAPPEARS."
	AndOr DragonCaveFlags 2 DragonCaveFlags
	Goto LeaveCave

LeaveCave:
	NewECL 25

PressReturn:
	HMenu Scratch1 1 "PRESS <RETURN> OR BUTTON TO CONTINUE"
	Return

	Save 1 SelMemorized13
	Exit

	Exit

	Save 1 In3D
	Exit
