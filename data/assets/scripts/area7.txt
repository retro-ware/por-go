SecretDoorRoll:
	LoadCharacter Scratch10
	Compare SelStatus 0
	IfEq
	Goto SecretDoorNextMember
	Save 1 Scratch9
	Compare Search 0
	IfEq
	Arith 1 Scratch9 Scratch9
	Compare SelRace 2
	IfEq
	Arith 1 Scratch9 Scratch9
	Compare SelRace 4
	IfEq
	Arith 1 Scratch9 Scratch9
	Random 5 Scratch8
	Compare Scratch8 Scratch9
	IfLe
	Goto SecretDoorFound

	Arith Scratch10 1 Scratch10
	Compare Scratch10 8
	IfLt
	Goto SecretDoorRoll
	Exit
