LoadEncounter:
	GetTable EncMonsterDistTable Scratch1 MonsterDist
	GetTable EncMonsterTable Scratch1 AuxRoll
	GetTable EncMoraleTable Scratch1 AuxMonster
	GetTable EncCountTable Scratch1 AuxEncResult
	GetTable EncPictureTable Scratch1 AuxEncResult2
	if Scratch6 != 0: Goto LoadEncounterGroup
	ClearMonsters
	Save 1 Scratch6

LoadEncounterGroup:
	LoadMonster AuxRoll AuxEncResult AuxEncResult2
	if AuxPartyStrength == 0: Goto EncounterCombat
	Add 1 Scratch1 Scratch1
	Sub 1 AuxPartyStrength AuxPartyStrength
	Goto LoadEncounter

EncounterCombat:

	Combat
	Gosub FleeIfLost
	OnGoto Scratch1 8 FrogsBeaten InsectsDone FerranBeaten ClearExit PatrolBeaten ClearExit ClearExit OrcRaidDone
	Exit // extra: the original runs on into data at AEF6
